Support

Have a question or need help? Check the FAQ below or reach out directly.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Export Data. You can export workouts, exercises, templates, measurements, or everything as CSV, JSON, or Excel files. Multiple exports are bundled into a ZIP file.

How do templates work?

Create a reusable workout blueprint, then start a session from it with one tap. You can duplicate and customize templates whenever your program changes.

Does WorkX collect my data?

Your training data is stored on your device and may be synchronized through iCloud when you enable that service. Optional exercise suggestions send the exercise details you submit to a cloud service. Optional feedback can include your message, attachments, anonymous service identity, and device or app metadata. WorkX does not use advertising or cross-app tracking.

How do I sync with Apple Health?

Go to Settings > Apple Health. WorkX can export completed workouts as strength training activities and sync body weight measurements in both directions. You'll be asked for permission the first time.

Can I use WorkX without an Apple Watch?

Yes. The Apple Watch companion app is optional. You can use all features of WorkX on your iPhone without a watch.

Can WorkX suggest exercise details?

When you choose the optional suggestion feature while creating an exercise, the exercise details you submit are sent to a cloud service to generate a suggestion. You can review and edit every suggestion before saving it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Overview

WorkX Fitness ("WorkX", "the app") is designed with privacy as a core principle. WorkX is focused on workout planning, strength tracking, templates, progress, Apple Watch, and optional Apple Health and progress-photo features.

Data Storage

Workouts, exercises, templates, measurements, and progress photos are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. When iCloud synchronization is enabled, eligible app data may also be synchronized through Apple's iCloud service.

No User Account

WorkX does not require a traditional user account. Optional cloud features use an anonymous service identity to operate and rate-limit those requests; this is not a public profile or sign-in account.

Optional Cloud Features

When you request an exercise suggestion, the exercise name and configuration you submit are sent to a cloud-based service hosted on Supabase to generate that suggestion. When you submit feedback, your feedback text, any attached screenshot, anonymous service identity, and device or app metadata are sent to Supabase and then to WorkX's private feedback system on GitHub. These features are optional.

Apple Health Integration

WorkX can integrate with Apple Health (HealthKit) to read and write body weight measurements and to export completed workouts as strength training activities. This integration is optional and requires your explicit permission. Data shared with Apple Health is governed by Apple's privacy policies.

Apple Watch

If you use the WorkX Apple Watch companion app, workout data is synced between your iPhone and Apple Watch over a direct, private connection (WatchConnectivity and HealthKit Workout Mirroring). No data passes through external servers.

Analytics and Tracking

WorkX does not use advertising or cross-app tracking. It does not include an analytics SDK. Device and app metadata may be included only when you voluntarily submit feedback, as described above.

Third-Party Services

Supabase processes the optional exercise-suggestion and feedback requests described above. Submitted feedback is also routed to WorkX's private feedback system on GitHub. Apple provides optional HealthKit, iCloud, and Apple Watch services under Apple's own policies.

Data Deletion

You can delete local app data by deleting individual records or uninstalling the app. Feedback you choose to submit is handled through the optional cloud feedback flow; contact us if you need help with a submitted feedback item.

Children's Privacy

WorkX is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact
