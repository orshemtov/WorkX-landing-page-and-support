Support

Have a question or need help? Check the FAQ below or reach out directly.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Export Data. You can export workouts, meals, exercises, or everything as CSV, JSON, or Excel files. Multiple exports are bundled into a ZIP file.

How does AI nutrition estimation work?

When you log a meal using text or a photo, WorkX sends that input to a cloud service that estimates the calories and macronutrients. The meal data is processed in real time and is not stored on any server. You can also log meals manually without using the AI feature.

Does WorkX collect my data?

No. All your workout, nutrition, and measurement data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. There is no user account, no analytics SDK, and no tracking. The only data that leaves your device is meal text or photos sent for AI nutrition estimation, which is processed in real time and not stored.

How do I sync with Apple Health?

Go to Settings > Apple Health. WorkX can export completed workouts as strength training activities and sync body weight measurements in both directions. You'll be asked for permission the first time.

Can I use WorkX without an Apple Watch?

Yes. The Apple Watch companion app is optional. You can use all features of WorkX on your iPhone without a watch.

How do I set nutrition goals?

Go to Settings > Nutrition Goals. You can set calorie and protein goals as fixed values, ranges, or body-weight multipliers. Your progress is shown on the daily meal view.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

Overview

WorkX Fitness ("WorkX", "the app") is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your fitness and nutrition data belongs to you and stays on your device.

Data Storage

All data you create in WorkX — including workouts, exercises, meal logs, body measurements, and progress photos — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is not transmitted to any external server, with the sole exception described in the "AI Nutrition Estimation" section below.

No User Account

WorkX does not require or offer user account creation. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile stored on any server.

AI Nutrition Estimation

WorkX offers an optional AI-powered nutrition estimation feature. When you use this feature, the text or photo you provide for meal logging is sent to a cloud-based service (hosted on Supabase) for real-time processing. This data is used solely to generate calorie and macronutrient estimates and is not stored on the server after processing is complete. You can choose to log meals manually without using the AI feature.

Apple Health Integration

WorkX can integrate with Apple Health (HealthKit) to read and write body weight measurements and to export completed workouts as strength training activities. This integration is optional and requires your explicit permission. Data shared with Apple Health is governed by Apple's privacy policies.

Apple Watch

If you use the WorkX Apple Watch companion app, workout data is synced between your iPhone and Apple Watch over a direct, private connection (WatchConnectivity and HealthKit Workout Mirroring). No data passes through external servers.

Analytics and Tracking

WorkX does not include any analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, advertising frameworks, or user tracking mechanisms. No usage data, device identifiers, or behavioral information is collected or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used by WorkX is the cloud-based AI nutrition estimation service described above. No other third-party services receive your data.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting individual records within the app or by uninstalling the app entirely. There is no server-side data to request deletion of.

Children's Privacy

WorkX is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact
